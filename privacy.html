Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Napolian Family Discord application ("the Application") may process information when you interact with or use the Application.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process only information that is reasonably necessary for the Application to function.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and how the Application is used, the Application may process information made available through Discord.

This may include:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Discord channel IDs.
  • Discord role IDs.
  • Information required to execute commands.
  • Configuration information required by server administrators.
  • Other information necessary for specific Application features.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Application does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from users unless a specific feature explicitly requires it and appropriate notice is provided.

Users should not provide passwords, payment information, authentication codes, or other sensitive information through the Application.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing the Application's features.
  • Processing commands.
  • Maintaining server-specific configurations.
  • Preventing abuse and misuse.
  • Debugging technical problems.
  • Maintaining and improving the Application.

5. Data Storage

Depending on the Application's features, certain information may be temporarily or permanently stored to allow the Application to function correctly.

The exact information stored may change as features are added or removed.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

When information is no longer necessary, it may be deleted or anonymized where reasonably possible.

7. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure required to operate the Application, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the Application.

8. Discord

The Napolian Family operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own privacy policies.

Users should review Discord's policies to understand how Discord handles their information.

9. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by the Application.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. User Requests

If you have a question about information processed by The Napolian Family, you may contact the Application owner through the official Discord server or the available contact method associated with the Application.

11. Children's Privacy

The Application is not intended to request or collect information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using the Application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Application, its features, or applicable requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or The Napolian Family, you may contact the Application owner through the official Discord server or the available contact method associated with the Application.
